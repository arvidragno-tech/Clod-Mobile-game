# Art bible

The visual rules for *UNDERCITY: RIPPER*. All shipped art is generated by
reproducible Python scripts (`tools/art/`), so these rules are enforced in code.
A human artist can replace any PNG 1:1 as long as they keep the size, density
and palette conventions below.

## Direction

Modern, cinematic pixel art: dark, wet, and lit by practical light sources
(lamps, monitors, neon, emergency beacons). The aim is detail through
lighting and material, not noise. It is an original setting, so don't borrow
any existing franchise's logos, typography, characters or architecture.

## Pixel density

**One global density: 1 art pixel = 3 canvas units** (`GameConfig.PIXEL_SCALE`).
The design canvas is 1920×1080, so the screen is 640×360 art pixels. The
canvas is scaled uniformly to the window (`canvas_items`, aspect `keep`):
integer-perfect at 1920×1080 (3 screen px per art px) and 1280×720 (2 px);
1600×900 and 1366×768 are uniform fractional scales with nearest sampling,
so pixels stay sharp (never blurred); other aspect ratios are letterboxed.
Nothing is ever drawn at a different density inside the canvas. That rules
out mixed-res sprites, smooth scaling and rotated pixel art in the UI. The
only deliberate exception is the operating field, an integer zoom camera
(3×–6×, default = largest that fits) over the 160 px surgical site.

| Asset | Native size (art px) | Shown at |
|---|---|---|
| Clinic room (wide) | 400 × 224 | 3× (1200×672, cropped by its frame) |
| Main-menu key art | 640 × 360 | 3× (full screen) |
| Black-market den (wide) | 600 × 150 | 3× (header strip) |
| Surgery backdrop (wide) | 340 × 270 | 1× inside the field viewport |
| Surgical region close-ups | 160 × 160 | 1× inside the field viewport (3×–6× zoom) |
| Character portraits | 64 × 64 | 3× |
| Character bodies (clinic door) | 32 × 64 | 3× |
| Cyberware icons | 32 × 32 | 3× (or 1× in field) |
| Tool icons | 24 × 24 | 3× |
| UI icons | 12 × 12 | 3× |
| Faction emblems | 16 × 16 | 3× |
| Mouse cursors | 16 × 16 | 2× hardware cursor (32 px) |
| UI frames (9-slice) | drawn at 3× | 1× |

How the density is enforced in the engine:
- **The surgical field** renders in a `SubViewport` whose container uses `stretch_shrink` = the field zoom, so field drawing happens at native art resolution and is then integer-scaled.
- **Icons and backgrounds** go through `PixelArt.scaled(path)`, which does a nearest-neighbour ×3 upscale (cached), shown with `TEXTURE_FILTER_NEAREST`; the root viewport's default canvas filter is nearest (Settings can switch to linear).
- **Fonts:** Silkscreen at 8 px for text inside the field, and VT323 / Pixelify Sans for the UI.

## Semantic colour

Colour carries meaning. Use these consistently across UI, FX and sprites.

| Meaning | Colour | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technology / information | Cyan | `2ee6c8` | Tech glows, HUD accents, scans, wires, splice terminals |
| Stable / success | Green | `6dff8a` | Stable vitals, success, neural band "stable", OK checks |
| Warning | Amber | `ffb43d` | Caution states, money, pain, instability |
| Danger | Red | `ff4d4d` | Critical vitals, failure, abort, complications |
| Black market / illegal | Magenta | `ff3d81` | Undermarket UI, unknown properties, brand accents |
| Experimental | Electric blue | `3a8cff` | Tier 4, rare items |
| Blood | Deep red ramp | `b3122e` | Field blood, never UI |

Rarity colours are common `8e9aa8`, uncommon green, rare blue, epic `c46aff`
and legendary magenta.

## Ramps and shading

- **8-step hue-shifted ramps** (`palette.ramp()`): shadows shift cooler, highlights warmer, and the base sits at index 4. Skin, hair, cloth, metal and glow all use ramps.
- **Light direction:** key light from the upper left, plus a cool rim light (`3b9da0`, fixed index 7) on the opposite edge, used sparingly.
- **Dithering:** an ordered 4×4 Bayer, only inside a narrow band between two shades (`dither_band`). It is never full-surface noise. Light falloff is banded and dithered (`LitScene`).
- **Outlines:** selective outline (sel-out): a dark outline where the sprite meets the background, lightened where light hits. Interior lines use the darker ramp steps, not pure black.
- **Specular:** 1–2 px highlights on metal, lenses and wet tissue. Glow cores are near-white and fall off through the glow ramp.

## Materials

| Material | Rule |
|---|---|
| Skin | 7 base tones (`SKIN_TONES`), heightfield-shaded, low-contrast dither at the shadow edge |
| Chrome (T1–2) | Cool grey ramp, bright specular |
| Gunmetal (T3, military) | Dark, low specular, amber or red status LEDs |
| Ceramic (T4, experimental) | Pale, blue emissive seams |
| Prototype (T5) | Mixed materials, magenta or blue cores, exposed circuitry |
| Brass | Cheap or improvised hardware |
| Blackened | Black-market metal with magenta accents |
| Tissue | `TISSUE` ramp, wet highlights. Necrotic tissue shifts toward purple |
| Nerves | Yellow `NERVE` ramp, 1 px wide with a highlight |

Cyberware tier reads at a glance: T1 is simple and chunky; T5 is dense, glowing and asymmetric.

## Characters

Characters are composited at runtime from **palette-indexed parts**:
- Each pixel is encoded as R = ramp × 32 and G = shade × 32, using these ramps:
  0 skin, 1 hair, 2 cloth A, 3 cloth B, 4 metal, 5 glow, 6 iris, 7 fixed.
- `CharacterArt` recolours each part with the customer's palette from `data/palettes.json` and caches the result (up to 48 textures).
- Layer order (back to front): hair_back, body, outfit, head, facial hair, scar, tattoo, cyber_low (under hair), hair, cyber, accessory.
- Portrait variety comes from:
  - 3 builds, 3 head shapes × 4 expressions
  - 9 hair styles and 12 outfits
  - facial hair, scars, tattoos
  - 11 cyber pieces and 9 accessories
  - 7 skin tones and 12 hair colours, cloth and metal palettes

  Archetypes (street merc, gang member, corporate, smuggler, dock worker, bounty hunter, fixer, criminal, civilian, black-market tech) pick from their own outfit and accessory pools.
- Installed chrome shows on the character: eye and visor cyber in portraits; arm, legs, spine and eye on the clinic-door body sprite.

## Environments

- **Clinic tiers 0–5** (basement → backroom → underground → professional → advanced → elite) share one camera and layout, so the upgrade reads as the same room getting better: the light gets cleaner, the equipment richer and the colour more controlled.
- **Black market:** a flooded tunnel lit in magenta, with tape-patched panels, a signal-tear glitch shader and corrupted text. It should never look like the clinic.
- **Surgery:** three backdrops (by clinic tier band) with lamp cones, monitors and robotic arms. Lighting is drawn additively (`SurgeryLighting`) with dithered falloff and a red emergency beacon during complications.

## UI

- 9-slice pixel frames (panels, cards, HUD, toast, danger, black-market) and button styles with four states each (normal / hover / pressed / disabled), drawn at 3× (`tools/art/ui_art.py`), plus a cyan keyboard-focus frame. The theme is built by `tools/build_theme.gd`.
- Controls are at least 40 canvas units tall (the tests enforce on-screen placement); primary actions are 56–72. Desktop layouts use panels side by side instead of stacked cards.
- Monitors use ECG strips, segmented meters and pixel fonts. Medical readouts use green, amber and red as above.

## FX

Pixel particles (square, integer-sized), floating text, a shake of at most
10 px, flashes, and haptics. At most 12 live emitters
(`FxLayer.MAX_EMITTERS`). Blood splats are capped at 90.

## Regenerating

```sh
python3 tools/generate_art.py                 # everything (~5 s)
python3 tools/generate_art.py chars tools     # groups: palette chars cyberware tools regions env ui
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/build_theme.gd
```

## Final direction (1.0)

The final style combines high-detail pixel art, dark cyberpunk, neon
futurism, advanced medical technology and industrial science fiction. It is a
modern premium pixel look, not retro 8-bit.

### Lighting roles

| Role | Colour | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Key light | White surgical (`e8fff8`) | Operating lamps, ring lights, field lamp pool |
| Ambient | Dark blue/purple | Scene ambient for every backdrop (0.2–0.4 blue-purple) |
| Technology | Cyan | Monitors, holo panels, implant cores, confirmations |
| Black market | Magenta | Undermarket, hidden stashes, unknown properties |
| Warning | Amber | Door work lights, minor mistakes, caution |
| Critical | Red | Emergency beacon, major mistakes, flatline |

Dark areas stay dark. Glow comes only from emissive pixel clusters with
banded, dithered falloff (`LitScene`), and nothing glows unless it is a light
source.

### Environmental storytelling (clinic tiers)

`tools/art/props.py` adds set dressing to every tier, and the cheap clinics
look worse:
- power boxes with breaker LEDs and HV labels; tier 0 has a sparking wire
- vents with grime streaks
- biohazard bins that overflow at low tiers
- graffiti tags: "NO DOC", "R4T", "XX"
- old and fresh blood
- discarded implants with dying LEDs
- instrument carts and diagnostic carts
- floor cables and drains
- field repairs in grey tape
- hidden stash compartments leaking magenta light: a wall panel at tiers 0–1, a floor hatch from tier 2
- foreground boxes or hard cases, so the floor plane is never empty

### Animation

All animation moves in whole art pixels, and **Reduce Motion** turns off
everything that isn't essential.

- **Clinic:** the generator exports anchors to `data/clinic_fx.json` for:
  - blinking LEDs, monitor refresh lines and blackout flickers, ECG traces
  - holo shimmer, spinning vent fans, steam, sparks, drips, blinking robot-arm lasers

  The customer breathes (upper body rises 1 px). Dust drifts in the lamp light.
- **Portraits:** eyes blink every few seconds. This is skipped automatically when optics or visors cover the eyes.
- **Cyberware icons** (`CyberwareIcon`):
  - every icon: emissive pulse and a scan glint
  - optics: a refocus blink
  - neural and spine: data flicker
  - limbs: a faster servo glint
- **Surgery:**
  - lamps flicker on at the start
  - neural signals travel along exposed nerves
  - the wound flushes with each heartbeat
  - mistakes get rings in the semantic colours
- **Menu:** key art (`ui/title_keyart.png`) with rain, a buzzing cross sign, a pulsing door light, a blinking optic and street steam.

### App icon

The icon is designed on a **60 px grid**:
- `assets/windows/app_icon.ico` holds 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px images, nearest-scaled from the grid (240 px is an exact 4×; 1024 px is 17× with a 2 px bleed).
- It uses big, high-contrast shapes (cyan implant, magenta-edged scalpel, blood drop) so it reads at 16–32 px in the taskbar.

### QA rules (checked in screenshots)

- One density everywhere (1 art px = 3 canvas units). Wide scenes (clinic, key art, den, surgery backdrop) were redrawn at the new width rather than stretched.
- No anti-aliasing: every `StyleBoxFlat` has `anti_aliasing = false`, textures use the nearest filter, and soft shadows are not allowed.
- Text never relies on colour alone.
