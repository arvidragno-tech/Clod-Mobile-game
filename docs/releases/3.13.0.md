## What's new in 3.13.0

**Stability and saves**
- An operation that ends (finished or aborted) no longer leaves a surgical dilemma open. This was the cause of the rare "shift 10: no dilemma left open" stability failure.
- A conversation open when the game closes is kept and resumes on its line. Decisions that arrive together queue instead of replacing each other, and appointments wait their turn instead of being skipped.
- Saves from 3.10.0, 3.11.0 and 3.12.0 load in this build. The save format is unchanged (version 5).

**Economy**
- A choice that spends money, supplies, parts or tokens needs them. This applies to events, street encounters and COMMS replies, which now say what's missing. Rook's favour, Hester's tip-off and the crew's reprisal no longer pay out for nothing.
- A fine or cost you can't refuse takes the cash you have and puts the rest on your debt, the same way everywhere. It always shows in full in the ledger.
- Decision cards show the standing, heat and street reputation you will really get.

**Surgery**
- Every implant category has its own operation, with a signature step and its own mistake:
  - neural: map the cortex
  - spine: thread the bus
  - arms: tension the tendons
  - legs: load-test the knee
  - eyes: hold the gaze
  - dermal: lay the plates
  - respiratory: seal the airway
  - internal: clamp the vessels

**Cutscenes**
- All eight scenes are rewritten as six shots each and run 38 to 42 seconds when left to play. They were measured in the running game. SKIP and ESC still end a scene at once and mark it seen once.

**Graphics**
- The operating field is draped, prepped with iodine and clamped, with an opening shaped to each site.
- The diagnosis scan shows a skeleton, organs and nerves.

**Known limitations**
- The graphics pass covers only the surgery and diagnosis screens. Characters, clinic rooms, streets, the city map, icons and cutscene backgrounds are unchanged.
- This Windows build was built and smoke-tested automatically on a GitHub Windows runner, which has no GPU and uses software rendering. It has not been played by hand on Windows hardware.
- Tests run on Linux under Godot 4.3 with every push.
