## What's new in 3.14.0

A full graphics overhaul. The palette is the same; everything is drawn with far more pixels and detail.

**Characters**
- Cel-shaded with a dark ink contour, crisp eyes with a lash line and catch-light, lips and noses drawn in light and shadow, and hair in clumps with a glossy crown.
- Portraits and bodies have twice the pixels (288x336 and 120x240), smoothed edges and a rim light, with faces kept at the same size.

**Scenes**
- Clinic rooms, their upgrades, every street view, the title screen and the black market are painted at twice the pixel count, with fine detail: panel seams, rivets, labels, screens, neon tubes, cables and grime. The rooms have new clutter.
- The city map is three times as detailed, with rooftops, water tanks and lit windows.

**Surgery**
- The operating field is drawn at four times the detail: anatomy, implants, and a new table surround with drape and clamps, instrument trays, gauze, a vitals monitor, an IV bag and robot arms. Zoom steps 2x / 4x / 6x.
- Implant, tool, UI and faction icons are drawn at their display size. Implants are redrawn with plates, pistons and screw heads.

**Cutscenes**
- Seven new full-screen illustrations (the flooded towers, the stairs down to the clinic, cars in the rain, the table under the lamp, the lattice in its tray, the notes wall, the cash drawer), drawn at the size of the screen.

**Fixes**
- No windows showing through buildings or doors, no drips outside the tunnel arch, no clipped implant edges or floating tape, no overlapping signs on the title screen.

Gameplay, scoring, tolerances and implant placement are unchanged. Nothing in saves changed: the format is still version 5, so 3.13 saves load as before.

**Known limitations**
- This Windows build was built and smoke-tested automatically on a GitHub Windows runner, which has no GPU and uses software rendering. It has not been played by hand on Windows hardware.
- Tests run on Linux under Godot 4.3 with every push.
